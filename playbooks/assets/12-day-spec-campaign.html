04 / 10
Day 01 · Email one

The heavy lifter

Your full spec. Everything else in the cadence is follow-up. Anatomy:

Opener
"I know · I heard · I saw" — pick the strongest signal you've got on the company or the manager.
Body
FAB the candidate — Feature, Advantage, Benefit. The Benefit is the line that gets the reply.
Close
A single, specific call-to-action. Not "let me know." Something they can say yes to in one click.

Days 03 · 04 · 07

Short. Purposeful.
Multi-channel.

DAY 03
Email #2. A one-line bump referencing the candidate and why they're relevant. Nothing more.
DAY 04
LinkedIn Touch #1. Send the same spec via LinkedIn message. Different inbox, same offer.
DAY 07
LinkedIn Touch #2. Short follow-up on LinkedIn. Keep it visible without being noise.

Length kills response rate on bumps. The first email did the heavy lifting. These keep you in the inbox.

Day 08 · Email three

The proof email

The third email introduces social proof. Pick one:

OPTION 01
A one-line client testimonial from a similar placement.
OPTION 02
A recommendation or recognisable name in the candidate's reference list.
OPTION 03
A second relevant candidate — if you have one, two is a pattern, not a pitch.

Something that says "others trust me with this" without you having to claim it.

Day 12 · Email four

The break-up
(that opens the next door)

Clean exit. Signal you'll follow up in three months. Then ask the one question that turns a closed conversation into an open one:

Is there anyone else in the business worth speaking to?

Even a no is intelligence.
